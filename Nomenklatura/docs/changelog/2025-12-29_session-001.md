### 2. Fixed Redaction Display in Character Card View

**File Modified:**
- `Nomenklatura/Views/Dossier/CharacterCardView.swift`

**Issue:** The service record displayed a redaction block (`████████`) randomly inserted between two unrelated sentences, making it look misplaced rather than intentionally redacting content.

**Before:**
```swift
text.append(redacted)  // Random insertion
let rest = AttributedString(generateServiceRecordSuffix())
text.append(rest)
```

**After:**
```swift
let transitionPrefix = AttributedString(" Prior assignment at ")
text.append(transitionPrefix)

var redacted = AttributedString("████████")
redacted.backgroundColor = theme.inkBlack
redacted.foregroundColor = theme.inkBlack
text.append(redacted)

let transitionSuffix = AttributedString(" terminated following review. ")
text.append(transitionSuffix)
```

**Result:** Redaction now reads naturally: "Prior assignment at ████████ terminated following review."

## Technical Notes

### Relative Date System
The codebase now uses the following conventions for dates:

**Age Categories:**
- `"elderly"` - Characters who were adults before the Revolution
- `"middle-aged"` - Characters who came of age during the Revolution
- `"young"` - Characters born around the Revolution
- `"very young"` - Characters born after the Revolution

**Era Strings:**
- `"before the Revolution"` - Pre-revolutionary period
- `"during the Revolution"` - Civil War period
- `"during the Purges"` - Consolidation period
- `"after the Purges"` - Current era
- `"years after the Purges"` - Recent past
- `"pre-Revolution"` - For education/career entries

### Preserved Historical Dates
Real historical events that predate the alternate history divergence retain their actual dates:
- 1918 flu pandemic
- 1890s homesteading
- Great Depression references
