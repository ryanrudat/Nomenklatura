### 4. Fix Treasury Starting in Critical Condition

**Problem:** Treasury dropped to critical within 1-2 turns of starting a new game.

**Root Causes:**
1. Stat drift penalized treasury when below 45 (the starting value!)
2. Economy expenses were too high relative to income
3. Treasury was being hit twice (drift + economy system)

**Files Modified:**

#### `Services/GameEngine.swift` - Remove Treasury from Stat Drift

```swift
// Before: Treasury included in drift stats
let driftStats = [
    ("stability", game.stability),
    ("popularSupport", game.popularSupport),
    ("treasury", game.treasury)  // This was wrong!
]

// After: Treasury removed (has its own economy system)
let driftStats = [
    ("stability", game.stability),
    ("popularSupport", game.popularSupport)
]
```

Also adjusted thresholds:
- Crisis drift: < 25 (was < 30)
- Moderate concern: < 35 (was < 45)

#### `Services/EconomyService.swift` - Rebalance Economy

| Expense | Before | After |
|---------|--------|-------|
| Base military | 15 | 8 |
| Base social | 10 | 6 |
| Infrastructure | regionCount × 2 | regionCount |
| Industrial regions bonus | +3 each | +2 each |

| Income | Before | After |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Industrial divisor | /5 | /4 (more income) |
| Agricultural divisor | /10 | /8 (more income) |
| Base loyalty modifier | 0.5 + 0.5× | 0.6 + 0.4× |
| Minimum production | 10 | 15 |

#### `Models/CampaignConfig.swift` & `Models/Game.swift` - Starting Values

| Stat | Before | After |
|------|--------|-------|
| Treasury | 45 | 55 |
| Food Supply | 40 | 45 |
