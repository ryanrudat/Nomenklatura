## TOP LEADERSHIP (Shared Track)

### Harold Mitchell
- **Position:** General Secretary (Index 8, Shared Track)
- **Faction:** Youth League
- **Origin:** Fitzgerald City, Zone 2
- **Background:** Working-class family. Father was a textile mill foreman.
- **Career:** Rose through Youth League organizing textile strikes. Caught Chairman Fitzgerald's eye. Was Fitzgerald's chosen successor.
- **Service Record:** Subject emerged from Youth League organizing activities in Fitzgerald City's textile districts. Distinguished himself during the labor struggles of the Depression era. Rose to prominence under Chairman Fitzgerald's direct mentorship. Appointed successor through established Party procedures.
- **Current Assignment:** Presides over the Standing Committee from the Mitchell Compound in the Capital. Chairs all Politburo sessions.

### General Raymond Carter
- **Position:** Deputy General Secretary (Index 7, Shared Track)
- **Faction:** Princelings
- **Origin:** Zone 3, Agricultural Zone (born); Fitzgerald City, Zone 2 (migrated)
- **Background:** Son of agricultural laborers. Escaped the harsh conditions of the collective farms.
- **Career:** Enlisted in revolutionary militia at start of Revolution. Rose through ranks during Civil War under General Steele's command. Wounded twice (Fitzgerald City, Highland). Married into the Briggs family.
- **Service Record:** Subject is a veteran of the Revolution, having served with distinction during the Civil War. Commanded forces in the Great Lakes offensive under General Steele. Wounded in action at the Battle of Fitzgerald City. Rose through military-political structures to highest command positions.
- **Current Assignment:** Oversees military affairs and coordinates with the Political Directorate of the People's Army.

## PARTY APPARATUS TRACK

### Eleanor Patterson
- **Position:** Second Secretary of the Central Committee (Index 6, Party Apparatus)
- **Faction:** Youth League
- **Origin:** South Red Harbor, Zone 5
- **Background:** working-class family. Father a longshoreman, mother a seamstress. Abandoned faith for Party.
- **Career:** Rose through Youth League as organizer and propagandist. Survived Purges by denouncing her mentor Professor Harrigan. Elevated by Mitchell over more experienced candidates.
- **Service Record:** Subject emerged from Youth League organizing in Red Harbor. Demonstrated exceptional propaganda capabilities. Advanced rapidly through Central Committee apparatus following the Purges. Selected for Second Secretary position through direct appointment by General Secretary Mitchell.
- **Current Assignment:** Manages daily operations of the Central Committee. Controls personnel appointments across the Party apparatus.

### Comrade Henderson

- **Faction:** Youth League
